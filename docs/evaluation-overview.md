# Evaluation overview

## Purpose

The owner defines /evaluation/ as the growing entry point for evidence of nomue's
usefulness from multiple angles, in an objective, inspectable and reproducible form.
Individual papers are contributions to that program, not its organizing identity.

The current entry points are decision quality, cost and time, and concrete cases.
Cases from an existing study are examples, not independent additional experiments.
Each dimension presents its question, observed result, tested scope, interpretation
and primary evidence link. Keep detailed methods and study-specific disclosures in
the corresponding Research article; keep the overview useful as further evidence
is added. Add dimensions when warranted by published evidence, without presenting
planned evaluation as a completed result or treating the current three as exhaustive.

Explain precisely what public materials reproduce. Recomputing aggregates or
rescoring saved answers does not imply independent replay of private product runs.
Keep studies with different models, tasks and endpoints separate.
