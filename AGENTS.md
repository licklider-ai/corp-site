# Repository work

Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the applicable policy linked from `README.md` before
editing. Public content follows `docs/publication-architecture.md`; product and
agent documentation also follows `docs/agent-readable-documentation.md`.

## Blog tasks only

For any Blog commission, outline, draft, revision, review, or Blog-specific site
integration, read `docs/blog/README.md` and follow `docs/blog/WRITE.md` before
drafting. This trigger applies regardless of where the draft is stored or whether
the public Blog routes exist. Ordinary Research, Engineering, News, Docs, homepage,
and product work does not inherit Blog's audience, article structure, or cadence.

The Blog policy defines editorial decisions; the writing procedure executes it.
Neither changes upstream product/scientific authority or authorizes publication.
