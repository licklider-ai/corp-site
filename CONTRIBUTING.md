# Contributing to corp-site

Follow the repository's documented publication architecture, build, and deployment rules.

## Artifact naming and provenance

Use durable product, task, and accountable-role terminology for repository
names, paths, titles, branch names, workflow labels, package metadata, and other
navigation surfaces. Avoid decorative attribution to a transient drafting,
review, or automation mechanism when it does not help a reader interpret or
reproduce the work.

This naming guidance is not a concealment rule. Preserve and disclose the
software, service, provider, model, or execution environment whenever it is
material to reproducibility, reliability, conflicts of interest, policy or
contract compliance, scientific interpretation, evidentiary integrity, or due
diligence. Do not erase required provenance and do not imply unsupported human
authorship. Put material process disclosure in the repository's appropriate
governance, methodology, review, or due-diligence record rather than relying on
promotional labels or autogenerated footers.

When provenance is required, record the accountable role, review scope,
independence boundary, inspected sources, date, relevant environment details,
and immutable revision or content hash. Preserve exact technical, scientific,
contractual, and evidentiary identifiers.
