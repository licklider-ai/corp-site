# Contributing to corp-site

Follow the repository's documented publication architecture, build, and deployment rules.

## Artifact and provenance hygiene

Repository artifacts must remain product- and task-centered. Repository and
directory names, filenames, document signatures and attribution footers,
comments, prompts, review records, provenance and other metadata, branch names,
commit messages and trailers, issue and pull-request text, workflow, job, check,
and artifact names, package and release metadata, and generated output must not
identify or imply the drafting, review, automation, or execution environment.

When provenance is required, record the accountable role, review scope,
independence boundary, inspected sources, date, and immutable revision or
content hash. Do not make an unsupported claim of human authorship. An
identifier required by the technical, scientific, contractual, or evidentiary
subject matter remains exact; this exception never exists merely to credit a
creation mechanism.
